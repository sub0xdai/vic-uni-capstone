## 1 Introduction


### 1.1 Background


Digital transformation has changed the way businesses connect with
customers, promote their products, and compete. Small businesses now use
websites, social media, and email marketing to reach their audiences and
maintain visibility. However, creating regular and engaging content
across different channels requires time, creativity, and skills that
many small business owners do not have.

Recent advances in Artificial Intelligence, especially Generative AI,
allow businesses to automate tasks such as content creation, customer
engagement, and data-driven communication. Generative AI can produce new
content and is rapidly transforming marketing by helping organisations
create personalised and scalable messages (Cillo & Rubera, 2024). These
developments show that AI can help businesses work more efficiently
while still connecting with customers in meaningful ways.

Even though AI tools are becoming more common, organisations often find
it difficult to integrate them into their regular marketing processes
because this requires changes to both technology and business operations
(Keegan et al., 2022). This highlights the need for simple solutions
that allow small businesses to benefit from AI without requiring
advanced technical skills.

Research also shows that using AI and digital technologies can improve
how companies operate and support better data-driven decision-making,
particularly when these tools are implemented through structured digital
platforms (Rosário & Raimundo, 2025). For small and medium-sized
businesses, adopting AI in digital marketing can support growth and
improve competitiveness in today’s markets (Liando et al., 2024). These
developments create an opportunity to design an AI-powered platform
tailored specifically for small businesses, enabling them to produce
marketing content more efficiently while using fewer resources.

### 1.2 Problem Statement


Digital marketing plays a key role in business success, but many small
businesses struggle to maintain a strong online presence. Creating
quality marketing content takes time, skill, and money—resources that
small businesses often do not have. Since owners handle many
responsibilities, it can be difficult for them to regularly plan, write,
and update content for blogs, social media, and email campaigns.

​

AI technologies can help automate marketing, but many businesses find
them hard to use. Integrating artificial intelligence into existing
processes can be complex and often requires technical skills that small
businesses may not have (Keegan et al., 2022). As a result, many AI
tools are too technical, too expensive, or not built for small business
needs.

​

Research shows that while AI-driven digital marketing can help small and
medium businesses grow and compete, these businesses often lack simple
tools to make the most of these benefits (Liando et al., 2024). This
leads to a gap between what AI marketing can offer and what small
businesses can actually use.

​

Small business owners need an easy, affordable platform to create
marketing content quickly, without advanced technical skills or a big
budget.

There are many AI-based content and marketing tools available today, but
most are made for large organisations or need a complex setup and
expensive subscriptions. Many current solutions focus on just one
feature, such as text generation or analytics, rather than offering a
simple, all-in-one workflow for small businesses. Because of this, small
businesses often find these tools hard to use or not a good fit for
their needs, underscoring the need for a user-friendly, affordable
platform designed for SMEs (Liando et al., 2024).

### 1.3 Proposed Solution


There are already many AI-powered content creation tools available, such
as ChatGPT-based writing assistants like Jasper and Copy.ai, as well as
marketing tools with built-in AI features. These tools show that more
businesses are seeking automated content and that AI is becoming
increasingly important in marketing. They can quickly create text,
handle various content types, and help marketing professionals work more
efficiently.

However, these tools often have drawbacks for small businesses. Many
expect users to know how to set up prompts, plan marketing strategies,
or handle technical details, which can be hard for people without a
background in these areas. Also, most are made for large companies or
serve as separate tools, not as part of a system designed for
small-business needs. As Keegan et al. (2022) point out, organisations
often struggle to integrate AI into their existing processes, especially
when the tools are not easy to use or the business is not ready for
them.

These issues show that current AI tools do not fully meet the real needs
of small and medium-sized businesses. Studies have found that while AI
marketing tools can help these businesses grow and compete, there are
still not enough easy-to-use options available (Liando et al., 2024).

To solve this problem, this project suggests creating an AI-powered
content generation tool made for small businesses. The tool will be a
cloud-based service that allows users to create professional marketing
content quickly and easily, even if they lack technical skills. Users
will enter key business details, such as brand voice, products, and
target audience. The system will then use this information to work with
a large language model to generate blog outlines, social media posts,
and email newsletters.

The platform will also include editing and analysis tools to assess tone
and keyword use, ensuring the content aligns with marketing goals. Using
AI and digital tools in this way can help organisations work more
efficiently and make better decisions (Rosário & Raimundo, 2025).

To maintain high-quality and relevant AI-generated content, the proposed
system will include a content evaluation module. This module will assess
generated text using predefined metrics such as readability scores,
keyword relevance, and tone consistency with the brand voice. For
example, readability will be measured using standard metrics like the
Flesch Reading Ease score, while keyword matching will ensure content
aligns with the business’s target audience and marketing goals. The
platform will also enable users to provide feedback through a simple
rating system. This feedback will help refine prompt structures and
enhance the quality of future outputs. By combining evaluation metrics
and user feedback, the platform offers a more structured and reliable
AI-assisted marketing workflow.

### 1.4 Project Aim and Objectives


> **Project Aim**
>
> The aim of this project is to develop an AI-powered
> Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platform that enables small businesses to
> efficiently generate high-quality, personalized marketing content. The
> platform will be simple to use and affordable, allowing businesses to
> improve their online presence and remain competitive without requiring
> advanced technical skills.
>
> **Project Objectives**
>
> To achieve this aim, the project will pursue the following objectives:

- Develop a system that allows multiple businesses to securely access
  and manage their own accounts within a shared platform environment.

- Design an easy-to-use interface that enables business owners to define
  their brand voice, products, and target audience without technical
  expertise.

- Implement a Content Generator capable of producing a variety of
  marketing materials, including blog outlines, social media posts, and
  email newsletters.

- Integrate the platform with a Large Language Model through prompt
  engineering to ensure generated content aligns with each business’s
  profile and marketing goals.

- Provide editing and content management tools that allow users to
  review, modify, and save AI-generated drafts before use.

- Include analytics features to evaluate tone and keyword relevance,
  helping businesses create content that matches their marketing
  strategies and audience needs, thereby supporting the adoption of
  AI-powered marketing (Liando et al., 2024).

### 1.5 Project Scope


This project will develop a prototype of an AI-powered
content-generation platform for small businesses. The goal is to show
how Generative AI can be used in a straightforward SaaS setup to help
with marketing content creation and management.

**In Scope**

The project will include these features:

- Build a secure platform that allows multiple businesses to create and
  manage their own accounts.

- Create a user interface that allows businesses to enter their brand
  details, product information, and target-audience information.

- Add a Content Generator that uses AI to create blog outlines, social
  media captions, and email marketing drafts. Connect the platform to a
  Large Language Model to generate content tailored for each business.

- Include a built-in editor that lets users review, edit, and save the
  content they create. Add basic analysis tools to check the tone and
  keyword relevance of the marketing materials.

- Deploy the platform in the cloud to simulate a SaaS model accessible
  via a web interface.

**Out of Scope**

To keep the project manageable within the timeline, these features will
not be part of the scope:

- Full automation of marketing campaigns or scheduling across external
  platforms.

- Integration with third-party CRM or advertising systems.

- Image, video, or multimedia content generation.

- Advanced analytics, such as real-time customer behaviour tracking.

- Commercial deployment or live production use beyond the prototype
  environment.

By setting these boundaries, the project stays focused on showing how
Artificial intelligence can be used for marketing content generation and
makes sure the goals can be met within the academic timeframe.

### 1.6 Significance of the Project


This project matters because it addresses a real problem for many small
businesses: maintaining consistent, high-quality digital marketing when
resources are tight. By using Generative AI on a straightforward,
easy-to-use platform, the system shows that advanced technology can help
small businesses with daily operations, not just large companies with
expert teams.

Using AI tools in marketing can boost productivity, help businesses make
better data-driven decisions, and improve how they connect with
customers when these tools are part of well-designed digital solutions
(Rosário & Raimundo, 2025). For small and medium-sized businesses,
adopting Artificial intelligence in marketing can help them grow and
stay competitive as markets become more digital (Liando et al., 2024).
This shows why it is important to create solutions that make AI easier
to use and more available to everyone.

This project offers an automated and flexible content creation system,
showing that AI can do more than just save time; it can help small
businesses take a bigger role in the digital economy. It also adds to
our understanding of how new Artificial intelligence technologies can be
used in real business settings to support innovation, growth, and
inclusion.
