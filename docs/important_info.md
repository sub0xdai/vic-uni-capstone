During NIT3003 (IT Capstone Project 1), you will select a project title, form a team of 2-4 students, and collaborate on designing and planning your project.

By the end of NIT3003, each team should have completed the design and planning phase for their capstone project.

After completing NIT3003, you will focus on project implementation, meeting with your team at least once a week.

Before commencing NIT3004 (IT Capstone Project 2), at least 80% of the project implementation must be completed. (A demonstration will be required during the first week of NIT3004.)

(At least 8 weeks between NIT3003 and NIT3004)

Maintain a progress diary during this period, as it will be assessed in NIT3004. After completing NIT3003, hold regular team meetings (at least once a week) and update your individual diary after each session until NIT3004 starts.
